About Snake Game

Our free Snake game brings the classic arcade experience to your browser. Steer the snake to eat food and grow longer without hitting walls or yourself. With 3 difficulty levels, high score tracking, and full mobile support, it's the perfect quick game for any device.

Features

  • 3 difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard
  • High score saved per difficulty
  • Keyboard (arrows + WASD) and touch controls
  • Swipe support on mobile
  • Pause/resume with Space
  • Smooth Canvas rendering
  • Works on desktop and mobile
  • 100% free, no signup, no download
